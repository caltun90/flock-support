Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a community?

Open the app, tap "I'm an Admin," then "Create New Community." Fill in your community details — name, type (church, sports club, school, association, or other), and your admin account info. You'll get a 6-character community code to share with members.

How do members join my community?

Share your 6-character community code with members. They open the app, tap "I'm a Member," enter their name, email, password, and the code. You'll receive an approval request that you can accept or decline.

How does the subscription work?

Flock is free for up to 10 members. To grow beyond that, upgrade to Flock Starter ($19.99/month) for unlimited members and all premium features. Manage your subscription from your device's App Store settings.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone → tap your name at the top → tap "Subscriptions" → select Flock Starter → tap "Cancel Subscription." Your access continues until the end of your current billing period.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password?" Enter your email and we'll send you a reset link. If you don't receive it, check your spam folder or contact us.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app → go to Profile → tap "Delete Account." This permanently removes your account and all associated data. The action cannot be undone.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All data is stored securely with industry-standard encryption. We never sell your data to third parties. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Why don't I see prayer features in my community?

Prayer features are designed for church communities. If your community is set up as a sports club, school, or association, prayer-related screens are hidden by design. To enable them, the admin needs to set the community type as "Church" when creating it.
